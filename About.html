Why this course exists

Good trades were losing work to worse ones with better websites.

I kept seeing the same thing everywhere I looked: brilliant tradespeople — the ones who turn up, do the job properly and get recommended — sat behind a tired Facebook page or no website at all, while someone half as good showed up first on Google and took the call.

And the lead platforms? Charging them a fortune to rent back their own reputation, then selling the same job to four other firms at once.

So I started Sitework.uk to fix it: proper, fast, mobile-first websites for UK trades, built with Claude and sold on a simple monthly retainer. It worked. Then people started asking me how — so I documented every step of it. The prompts, the lead sheets, the call scripts, the pricing, the contracts. That documentation became BuildAIModels.

Nothing in the course is theory. If it's in a module, it's because I use it in the agency. When the agency's process improves, the course gets updated — and you get every update for life.
