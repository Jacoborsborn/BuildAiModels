1. Terms of sale

BuildAIModels ("we", "us") sells online courses — currently the Web Design Agency Starter Kit and the Agency Kit + SEO Automation Kit bundle — as one-time digital purchases.

  • What you buy: lifetime access to the purchased course content through your account, including future updates to that course.
  • Payment: processed by Stripe. We never see or store your card details.
  • Access: granted automatically to your account immediately after successful payment.
  • Accounts: you're responsible for keeping your login credentials private. One account per buyer; accounts are personal and non-transferable.
  • "Lifetime": the lifetime of the platform. If we ever wind the platform down, we'll give at least 90 days' notice and a reasonable way to download the materials.

3. Refund policy

Under the UK Consumer Contracts Regulations 2013 you have a 14-day cancellation right on digital purchases, with one standard condition:

When you first open any paid module, you're asking us to supply the digital content immediately — and you acknowledge that this waives the 14-day cancellation right, as the regulations allow. That's why module 00 is free: you can judge the course properly before anything is charged or waived.
  • Within 14 days, paid content not accessed: full refund, no questions — email us.
  • Paid content accessed: the cancellation right is waived, but if something is genuinely broken or badly not as described, email us and we'll be reasonable.

Refunds go back to the original payment method via Stripe, usually within 5–10 working days.

4. Earnings disclaimer

The course documents a real system from a real agency, but we make no promise you will earn anything. Results depend on your effort, your consistency and your market. Any figures shown in the course are examples of what the system has produced in specific circumstances, not projections of what you will make. Nobody honest can guarantee income — treat anyone who does with suspicion.

The course also contains practical guidance on UK outreach rules (TPS/CTPS, PECR, GDPR). It is education, not legal advice — for legal advice about your specific situation, speak to a solicitor.

5. Privacy policy

We collect the minimum we need to run the platform:

  • Account data: your email, display name and password (hashed), stored with Supabase (our database and authentication provider).
  • Purchase records: what you bought and when, linked to your Stripe payment ID. Card details stay with Stripe.
  • Course progress: which lessons you've completed, stored in your browser and/or your account.
  • Analytics: cookieless, aggregate page analytics only — no advertising trackers, no third-party cookies, no selling of data. Ever.

Your rights

Under UK GDPR you can request a copy of your data, correction, or deletion at any time — email us. Deleting your account removes your personal data from the platform; Stripe retains transaction records as required by financial regulations.

Emails

We email you about your account, purchases and course updates. Course-update emails include an unsubscribe link; account and receipt emails are transactional and can't be opted out of while you hold an account.
